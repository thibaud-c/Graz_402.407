Two stops, one geographic question

How might public space support a short break during a walk? These example stops suggest observations to make, not findings.

1. A central square

Near Hauptplatz, look for places to sit and routes through the square. Who can stop without blocking others?

2. A park setting

Near Stadtpark, compare shade and seating. Visit at the same time of day before drawing conclusions about comfort.

Source note: approximate locations chosen for teaching. No field observations have been collected.
