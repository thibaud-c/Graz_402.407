Digital mediation

How digital maps shape our experience of places.

What does it mean?

Digital mediation means that digital technologies influence how we understand and use places. A web map selects which features to show and how to present them. Those choices can guide our attention and movement.

  • A prominent label can draw our attention to one place.
  • A suggested route can lead us past some places and away from others.

Look at Graz

Compare a web map of Graz with the City of Graz website. Which places are emphasised? Whose descriptions appear? What would someone miss if they relied on just one of these accounts?
