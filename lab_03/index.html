Datafication

When an experience of a place becomes a data record.

What does it mean?

Datafication means turning aspects of activities, experiences or places into data that software can store, sort or compare. A description of a favourite resting place might become a location, a category and a time.

This makes it easier to compare records and put them on a map. It also involves choices. Which categories are available? What details do they leave out? A point labelled shade is a limited account of a place, not the whole experience of being there.

What does the record leave out?

The tags retain one environmental feature and a broad time of day. This can help us compare records, but it does not explain why the narrator returns or how their experience changes. Which social, sensory and practical details disappear? Would someone else experience this place in the same way?

In your future PPGIS project, the questions and categories you choose will shape what can become data. A useful simplification needs an explanation of its limits.
